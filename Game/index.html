  • BonusesOncoming car ×1.5 · big rig ×1.25 · threading between two cars ×2 · sideswipe · drifting over 40 km/h.
    • 
          
  • ComboEvery pass raises the multiplier (up to ×). It drains if nothing happens for .

  • CrashA hard hit on traffic or a wall ends the run. Its points still count towards your best and your wallet.

  • ChaseStay over  for a few seconds and  police cars come after you. They are quicker than you, weave through the traffic, and the traffic pulls over for them.
    • 
          
  • HeatEvery  seconds the heat goes up (to ): more cars (up to ), faster and braver ones.
