What the game keeps on your device

Your nickname, chosen car and colours, settings and personal-best scores are saved in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. The game does not send them to a server of its own, and clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Multiplayer

Lobbies run through public MQTT relay servers run by third parties. When you host or join a lobby, your nickname, car, position and chat messages are sent through those relays to the other players, who can see them. Please do not use your real name or share personal details in a nickname, lobby name or chat.

Ads

This site shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar identifiers to deliver and measure ads, and, where you allow it, to personalise them. Ads appear in the side columns on wide screens and as short full-screen breaks at natural pauses, such as when you open the menu or press Play.

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings, where you can switch off personalised ads
  • aboutads.info, to opt out of personalised ads from other companies

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and some other places are asked for their consent before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy settings link or message on this site, where one is shown.
